## Decisions represented

- `request / draft / scoped refinement / compare / fixed version / review` is the visible production loop;
- the Stage and next executable action dominate over explanatory copy;
- the Studio switches between `Rápido`, `Dirigido` and `Pro` without changing document context;
- the Action Dock identifies its exact target and offers one primary CTA;
- compact mode removes the persistent Inspector before sacrificing the Stage or primary action;
- local App Shell, Production Rail and Button components are reused;
- no emoji is intentionally used; visual state relies on text, semantic color, geometry and accessible
  design-system icons.
